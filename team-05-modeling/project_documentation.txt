2. EDA

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. Target Variable (`OD_REV_USD`):

   - Mean Revenue: $687,759.72

   - Median Revenue: $155,913.76

   - Standard Deviation: $1,880,158.82

   - Skewness: 12.0566 (Indicates a long tail of high-value intercontinental routes).

   - Log-Transformation (`np.log1p`): Reduced skewness to -0.3114, normalizing the distribution.



B. Network & Route Economics:

   - Unique Origin & Destination Airports: 150 each.

   - Top Grossing Corridors: Dominated by intercontinental and transcontinental routes (e.g., `JFK -> LHR` at $4.58B, `LAX -> JFK` at $3.93B).

   - Passenger Volume & Yield: Cumulative passenger volume across the dataset reached 4.24 billion, yielding an average of $384.73 per passenger. Ultra-long-haul routes (e.g., DTW/DFW/SFO to PVG) exceeded $1,800 to $2,400+ per passenger.



C. Macro & Temporal Trends (YoY & Seasonality):

   - Pandemic Impact & Recovery: Total revenue plunged from $148.07B in 2019 to $43.24B in 2020 (-70%), recovering steadily to peak at $190.50B in 2024.

   - Monthly Seasonality: Consistent summer surges with June ($817.1k mean) and July ($784.0k mean) generating peak earnings, and February dipping to a seasonal low ($566.8k mean).



3. Feature Engineering

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Specific features were identified as the core predictive drivers:

- `OD_PAX` (Passenger Volume): The single strongest direct predictor, displaying a massive 0.759 correlation with revenue (`OD_REV_USD`).

- `ORG_AIRPRT` & `DES_AIRPRT` (Origin & Destination Airports): Categorical route identifiers one-hot encoded to capture specific hub economics and elite intercontinental yields.

- `DEP_MONTH` & `DEP_YEAR` (Temporal Components): Capture macro recovery trends and recurring seasonal cycles.

- Engineered Cyclical Features (`MONTH_SIN`, `MONTH_COS`): Sine and cosine transformations of departure months to preserve chronological continuity between December and January.

- Route Historical Average Revenue (`ROUTE_AVG_REV`): Historical aggregate baseline revenue per origin-destination pair to give tree models strong priors.
