## Needs PM clarification

We are **not** guessing these. Answers change which columns are safe to use (leakage) and how they are cleaned.

| column(s) | question for PM |
|---|---|
| load_factor, seats_remaining | Measured at search_date (when the fare was quoted) or at departure / a later snapshot? If later, they are leakage. Also, load_factor ≈ 1 − seats_remaining/aircraft_capacity (rows off by more than 0.01: 0). Is one simply derived from the other? |
| competitor_min_price | Which competitors are included, and is it captured at the same moment, for the same cabin and the same route as the AA quote? Why is it missing (no competitor on the route vs. scrape failure)? The two causes need different imputation. |
| base_fare, taxes_fees | Are they ever known before the total price is? The .md says total_price = base_fare + taxes_fees exactly, which makes them leakage. 60 rows break that sum. Is the error in total_price or in the parts? |
| total_price | Units and scope: USD? Per passenger? One-way or round trip? Is it the lowest available fare or the fare actually sold? |
| fare_booking_class | Every fare letter shows up in every cabin (e.g. 'Y' in Basic Economy). In real AA inventory a letter maps to one cabin. What does this column represent here, and is it set before or at purchase? |
| cabin | Labels in the data: ['Basic Economy', 'Business/First', 'Main Cabin', 'Premium Economy'] (the .md writes 'Business-First'). Are Business and First intentionally merged (e.g. 2-cabin vs 3-cabin aircraft)? |
| search_id | Is this meant to be a unique quote key? 50 ids repeat; all of them are exact duplicate rows. Are repeated searches real events or ingestion duplicates? |
| search_date / departure_date | Time zone of the dates, and is search_date a date only or truncated from a timestamp? This matters for a time-based train/test split. |
| departure_hour | Local time at the origin? For connecting itineraries, is it the first leg's departure? |
| distance_miles | Great-circle O&D distance even for connecting itineraries, or flown miles? |
| is_holiday_period | Exact date windows for 'Thanksgiving / late-Dec / July-4'. Are they defined on departure_date only? |
| is_weekend_departure | Confirm the Fri–Sun definition (some analyses use Sat–Sun). |
| aircraft_capacity | Total seats on the equipment, or seats in the quoted cabin only? For connections, which leg? |
| carrier | Always 'AA' in this file. Are partner / codeshare (e.g. AA-marketed, Envoy-operated) itineraries included or excluded? |
